— Projects 🛠️
Project 01

Orayan AI 🌿

Bridging the gap between complex medical language and everyday understanding — so no patient ever feels lost or afraid again.

Medical reports are full of confusing terms. Orayan AI reads the report and explains it clearly in Sinhala or simple English — like a knowledgeable friend talking to you.

PythonNLPSinhala TTSHealthcare AI
The Problem: Thousands of Sri Lankan patients receive medical reports they cannot understand, causing anxiety and delays in treatment.

The Solution: Orayan AI generates a plain-language explanation in Sinhala or simple English — identifying key terms, diagnosis highlights, and recommended actions in a calm tone.

Impact: Reducing patient stress, improving health literacy, empowering people to understand their own health. 💙

Full details and demo coming soon.
Project 02

ASTERIA — V1 🌟

Turning therapy into play — because healing should never feel like a burden, especially for a child.

A hand and body tracker built into a fun puzzle game designed for paralyzed or disabled children. Movement becomes gameplay — therapy becomes joy.

OpenCVMediaPipePythonGame Dev
The Problem: Physical therapy for disabled children is repetitive and exhausting. Many disengage, slowing recovery.

The Solution: MediaPipe + OpenCV tracks movements in real time. Children play by moving their hands or body — no physical controller needed.

Impact: Making rehabilitation joyful and effective. 🌈
Project 03

BAYMAX — 1.0 🩺

Your personal health companion — always available, always private, always on your side.

A voice and chat assistant that runs fully on your device. No internet, no data sharing. Just honest, simple health guidance whenever you need it.

Local LLMVoice AIPythonOffline
The Problem: Most AI health tools require internet and send data to servers. In rural Sri Lanka, connectivity is unreliable and privacy matters.

The Solution: BAYMAX — 1.0 runs locally — health Q&A, symptom checking, medication reminders — all offline, all private. 🔒
Project 04

SPEAK SILENCE 🤟

"They are speaking. You just couldn't hear them — until now."

A gesture recognition app for deaf and mute patients in hospitals — hand gestures converted into text and voice in Sinhala and English.

MediaPipeOpenCVScikit-learngTTSPython
The Problem: Deaf and mute patients cannot communicate with medical staff — leading to misdiagnosis and delayed care.

The Solution: 15 critical hospital gestures recognized from a self-collected dataset. Outputs text + reads aloud via gTTS in Sinhala or English.

Impact: Every patient deserves to be heard. 💜
